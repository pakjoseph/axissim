# M8.0 final acceptance addendum

This note supplements the preserved canonical run report at `results/m8.0-3c76beb/report.md`. It clarifies the scope of the quantitative results without editing or regenerating the accepted study artifacts.

## Break-even outputs

In each case, the DRAM and DMA energy terms are additive accounting components applied to the **same logical transfer-byte count**. They are not independent physical traffic measurements. The “maximum additional SRAM-energy budget” is the modeled energy difference available for **all additional unpriced residency-related costs**; it is not a measured SRAM energy value. The per-frame amount is only the arithmetic average across 30 completed frames.

| Sensitivity case | DRAM / DMA coefficient (pJ/B) | Logical bytes saved | DRAM term saved (µJ) | DMA term saved (µJ, same byte count) | Total additional unpriced SRAM-cost budget (µJ / 30-frame run) | Arithmetic average (µJ / completed frame) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Base | 15 / 1.5 | 394,400 | 5.9160 | 0.5916 | 6.5076 | 0.21692 |
| DRAM 0.5× | 7.5 / 1.5 | 394,400 | 2.9580 | 0.5916 | 3.5496 | 0.11832 |
| DRAM 2× | 30 / 1.5 | 394,400 | 11.8320 | 0.5916 | 12.4236 | 0.41412 |
| DMA 0.5× | 15 / 0.75 | 394,400 | 5.9160 | 0.2958 | 6.2118 | 0.20706 |
| DMA 2× | 15 / 3 | 394,400 | 5.9160 | 1.1832 | 7.0992 | 0.23664 |

The base result is `394,400 B × (15 + 1.5) pJ/B = 6,507,600 pJ = 6.5076 µJ`. First-frame traffic is 290,176 B in both arms. The aggregate steady-frame ledger is 290,176 B without residency and 276,576 B with residency; multiplied over the 29 post-first frames, the difference is 394,400 B. The CLI does not provide frame-indexed byte observations.

The factor-of-two coefficient cases are sensitivity calculations at declared inputs. They are not statistical uncertainty intervals or measured technology ranges.

## Finite-run timing and static-energy scope

For the 30 completed frames, the C++ model reports a 0.680000 µs makespan reduction, 0.657333 µs mean-latency reduction, and 0.306540 µs P99 reduction. Both arms report zero deadline misses out of 30 jobs. These P99 and deadline values describe only this finite model run; they are not estimates of general tail latency or deadline reliability.

All five supported static-power inputs were explicitly configured to zero, so the modeled static-energy difference is zero. This is not evidence of zero physical static or leakage energy.

No real-silicon latency, energy-efficiency, power, physical traffic, or architecture-superiority conclusion follows. The model omits resident-weight SRAM access energy and resident-store static/leakage cost. Whether actual incremental SRAM costs fall below the calculated threshold remains unknown.
